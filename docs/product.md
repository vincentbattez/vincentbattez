# Product Overview

Vincent Battez's personal portfolio website - a freelance **Senior Full-Stack
developer** based in Lille, France. Professional showcase designed to attract
clients for senior-level full stack development work.

## Positioning

The role is **Senior Full-Stack developer**, not AI Engineer. AI is the *way of
working*, not the service sold.

The differentiator: he codes **with** AI daily, and argues that this makes
**architecture more critical, not less**. Code generation made implementation
fast and cheap; system boundaries did not become easier. At that speed, a
design mistake compounds into months of rework. Hence the site's core argument:
AI-speed without architecture and deep understanding → technical debt → rewrite
in 6 months.

**Do not** rebrand as "AI Engineer", "développeur IA", or sell AI integration /
automation as a service. Do not drop the AI angle either - it is what separates
him from every other senior full-stack freelance.

## Content Guidelines
- **Language**: French content for local market targeting
- **Tone**: Professional, confident, showcasing senior expertise
- **Focus**: software architecture at AI speed, deep understanding of the whole
  chain (need → ticket → prod → docs), code quality and transmission
- **Call-to-actions**: Direct scheduling and CV viewing links

## Target Audience
- Companies needing freelance senior full stack development expertise
  - Tech leads or CTOs who have felt the cost of AI-generated code shipped
    without architecture
- Professional network connections in French market

## Development Principles
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Performance-optimized for professional presentation
- Accessibility compliance for broader reach
- SEO optimization for local discovery
